# Ethogram of instantaneous behaviours and their associated beahvioural categories. 
## Please note those with a '-' in the first column were not assigned a behavioural category due to their limited observational frequency. The quantity of observations is available in parentheses. When husbandry records confirmed an individual's presence but it was not visible at any point during the selected observation window, its behaviour was categorized as 'NV' (not visible). An NV designation indicates the crab was either likely burrowing or submerged for an extended period (i.e., longer than the 30-minute observation period), or had died within the last 24 hours.

| Behavioural Category   | Instantaneous Behaviour | Definition |
|------------------------|-------------------------|------------|
| foraging               | foraging                | Foraging for detritus and small organisms on mudflats or in shallow waters. Dactyl(s) is moving towards the mouth. |
| locomotion             | walking                 | Walking or running on the mudbank. |
| locomotion             | entering seawater       | Actively entering the seawater (not foraging). |
| locomotion             | exiting seawater        | Actively exiting the seawater (not foraging). |
| locomotion             | vertical extension      | Rotating the body on a pivot. This includes climbing up the crabitat wall to flip directions. |
| burrow activity        | maintaining burrow      | Digging and excavating burrows, carrying sediment to/from the burrow entrance. |
| burrow activity        | entering burrow         | Actively entering an uninhabited burrow. |
| burrow activity        | exiting burrow          | Actively exiting a burrow. |
| burrow activity        | protecting burrow       | Territorially standing over a burrow entrance, usually with the dominant claw extended. |
| conspecific contact    | attacking burrow        | An intruder crab is forcefully removing a burrow resident by establishing physical contact - such as pereopod pulling. |
| conspecific contact    | defending burrow        | A burrow resident is being forcefully removed by an intruder via establishing physical contact - such as pereopod pulling. |
| conspecific contact    | threat encounter        | Physical contact among individuals (typically interlocking dominant claws) which does not occur above a burrow entrance. |
| resting                | shoreline resting       | The crab is standing at or within the seawater edge. Often claws are positioned for protection or the crab is cleaning eyestalks, maxillipeds and pereopods in the seawater. |
| resting                | mudbank resting         | Resting on the mudbank (not in seawater or above a burrow). Often claws are positioned for protection or the crab is picking algae off its eyestalks, maxillipeds and pereopods. |
| submerged              | submerged               | The crab is fully submerged in the seawater. This includes remaining still (i.e., not "swimming"), so long as the crab is entirely submerged. |
| burrowing              | burrowing               | The crab is observed resting in its burrow. There are no conspecific interactions. |
| - (33 obs)             | bubbling/expelling CO2  | Expelling CO2 gas bubbles from gills during respiration, to reach homeostasis in a changing environment. |
| - (9 obs)              | mating                  | Courtship rituals involving tactile contact. This includes mating (mounting) attempts. |
| - (16 obs)             | moulting                | Shedding old exoskeleton for ecdysis. |
| - (30 obs)             | putative sleep          | The crab is sleeping outside its burrow (otherwise the behaviour is noted as burrowing), where movement is limited and eyestalks are lowered. |
| - (27 obs)             | tapping                 | Tapping on the substrate with pereopods for vibrational communication (courtship or agonistic). |
| - (0 obs)              | waving                  | Males raising and waving dominant claws to attract females or communicate with other males. |
| Not Visible (NV)       | Not Visible (NV)        | Not visible. Husbandry records confirm this crab’s presence, but it was not visible at this observation interval. This crab was most likely burrowing at this time, although it may also have been entirely submerged in the seawater deep end (out of sight, and did not appear in-frame at any point throughout the observation period). |
